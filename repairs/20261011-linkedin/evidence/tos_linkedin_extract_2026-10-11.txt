LinkedIn - extractos legales relevantes
Consultados: 2026-10-11 con el Chrome real del usuario (CDP 9222). Sin acciones de cuenta.

[1] User Agreement - https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement
Seccion 8.2 "Don'ts" (texto original en ingles):
"...Develop, support or use software, devices, scripts, robots or any other means or
processes (such as crawlers, browser plugins and add-ons or any other technology) to
scrape or copy the Services, including profiles and other data from the Services;
Override any security feature or bypass or circumvent any access controls or use limits
of the Services (such as search results, profiles, or videos); ..."

[2] Crawling Terms and Conditions - https://www.linkedin.com/legal/crawling-terms
(version 25/05/2017; texto original en ingles):
"...through automated means, such as through bots, robots, spiders, crawlers, or
scrapers ("Automated Crawling & Indexing") ... Automated Crawling & Indexing without the
express permission of LinkedIn is strictly prohibited. By engaging in permitted
Automated Crawling & Indexing you agree to abide by these Terms and Conditions. You
agree to engage in Automated Crawling & Indexing only of the data, paths, and
directories authorized by LinkedIn. You agree: that you will not violate the
restrictions in any robot exclusion header; ... your use of data you collect through
Automated Crawling & Indexing will be confined solely to search indexing for display in
a publicly available search engine on the Internet unless granted separate approval by
LinkedIn for alternative usage and display on the Internet. You agree that you will not
rent, lease, loan, trade, or sell any data collected through Automated Crawling &
Indexing ..."

[3] CONCLUSION OPERATIVA
- robots.txt (User-agent: *) prohibe todo el dominio; los TOS prohiben el acceso
  automatizado sin permiso expreso y limitan el uso permitido a indexacion de
  buscadores publicos, que NO es el caso de este proyecto (analitica interna).
- La via declarada de permiso es whitelist-crawl@linkedin.com (Crawling Terms).
- Cualquier backfill o ampliacion del scraping queda, por tanto, fuera de lo que
  robots.txt/TOS permiten y requiere decision explicita de la persona (RF-6).
